# Grandma Lou's Christmas recipes

Family notebook (fictional, for BOAR tests).

## French toast

Day-old bread, cut into thick slices. Soak in milk with condensed milk, dip in beaten egg and fry. Drain and roll in
sugar with **cinnamon**. Grandma always used two-day-old bread.

## Christmas stuffing

Butter, onion, diced bacon, raisins, chopped Brazil nuts and toasted breadcrumbs. Raisins are mandatory, according to
Grandma.

## Chicken salad

Shredded chicken, grated carrot, apple, celery, mayonnaise and potato sticks on top, added right before serving.
