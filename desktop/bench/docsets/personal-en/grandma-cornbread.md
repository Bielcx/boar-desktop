# Grandma Lou's cornbread cake

Family recipe (fictional, for BOAR tests). Grandma Lou baked it on Sundays.

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 2 cups of cornmeal
- 1 cup of flour
- 2 cups of sugar
- 1 cup of milk
- 1/2 cup of vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon of fennel seeds
- 1 tablespoon of baking powder

## Steps

1. Blend the eggs, milk, oil and sugar.
2. Pour into a bowl and mix in the cornmeal, the flour and the fennel seeds.
3. Fold in the baking powder last.
4. Bake in a greased pan in a preheated oven at **350 °F for about 40 minutes**.

Grandma's secret: the fennel seeds, and never opening the oven in the first 25 minutes.
