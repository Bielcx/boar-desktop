# Grandma Lou's milk flan

Family recipe (fictional, for BOAR tests).

## Caramel

Melt 1 cup of sugar in the pan itself over low heat until golden. Spread it up the sides.

## Flan

- 1 can of sweetened condensed milk
- The same can filled with milk
- 3 whole eggs

Blend everything for 2 minutes and pour into the caramelized pan.

## Baking

In a **water bath**, in the oven at 400 °F, for about 1 hour and 30 minutes. Let it cool and chill for 4 hours
before turning it out. Grandma used to say: little holes in the flan mean the oven was too hot.
