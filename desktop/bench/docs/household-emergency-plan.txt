Silva family emergency plan (last reviewed March 2026)

Water
We keep 12 liters of drinking water per person in the three blue barrels in the garage, next to the bicycles. That covers the four of us for about three days. The barrels are emptied and refilled twice a year, in March and in September, when the clocks change. To treat tap or rain water we use 2 drops of unscented household bleach per liter and wait 30 minutes before drinking. The ceramic filter lives in the kitchen cabinet above the sink.

Meeting points
If we get separated at home, we meet at the mango tree at the corner of Rua das Flores. If the neighborhood has to be evacuated, the meeting point is the parish hall of São José, two kilometers north. Grandma Lucia's house in Campinas is the out-of-town contact; her landline is written on the fridge.

Power
The generator is a 2.2 kW inverter model stored in the shed. We keep 20 liters of gasoline with stabilizer, rotated every six months. The freezer gets power first, then the router, then phone chargers.

Medicines
Pedro's asthma inhaler (salbutamol) has a spare in the red first-aid bag by the front door. The bag is checked on the first Sunday of every month. Expired items go to the pharmacy on Avenida Brasil.
